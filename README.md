I'm a full-stack developer and AI/ML engineer who ships end-to-end: React/Next.js frontends, Node.js and Python backends, and the ML or LLM layer that makes a product actually smart. I care about measuring before building — ablations over assertions, calibrated risk scores over vibes — and about keeping user data and trust intact along the way.

## What I focus on
- **AI / ML engineering** — LLM-orchestrated agents (multi-node workflows, tool use), applied ML (sensor-based classification, NLP benchmarking), and rigorous evaluation before shipping.
- **Full-stack web engineering** — React/Next.js on the frontend, Node.js/Express and Python/FastAPI on the backend, with Postgres, MongoDB, and Firebase as the data layer.
- **Security-first product thinking** — zero-knowledge proofs, scam/fraud triage, and privacy-preserving design baked in from the start, not bolted on after.
